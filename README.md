# VeckansMat

[Open VeckansMat](https://veckansmat.veckans-mat.workers.dev)

VeckansMat helps your family decide what to eat and keep track of what to buy. The app is in Swedish and works on phones, tablets, and computers.

Unlock the app with your family PIN. You can save favorite recipes, plan dinners from Monday to Sunday, and make a shopping list from the week's meals. Mark items as you shop, add anything you need, and group ingredients in the order that suits your store. Give each ingredient a section letter, name the sections, and arrange them to match your usual route through the shop.

Everyone using the shared PIN can see and change the family's recipes, meal plans, and lists. Keep the PIN within the family.
